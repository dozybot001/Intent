# Intent 7.2.0

Released 2026-10-30. Relations between Intents, Decision sources, a Flow page in IntHub, a paged timeline, and much faster pulls. Objects without relations keep exactly the 7.1 shape.

## Intent flow

- Five relations between Intents ([design](../EN/intent-flow.md)). Three say where a goal comes from: `--part-of`, `--derived-from` and `--succeeds`. Two say what constrains it: `--depends-on` and `--conflicts-with`.
  - Pass them to `itt intent create`, or add them later with `itt intent relate ID --depends-on OTHER`.
  - `itt intent unrelate ID OTHER` withdraws one. Records are append-only, and a withdrawn record gains `removed_at`.
- `itt intent done/cancel ID --into OTHER` ends a goal and records that OTHER succeeds it. A conflict between the two is resolved into that succession.
- Relation states are derived from the two statuses, never stored: blocked / satisfied / broken, unresolved / resolved, handover pending / handed over, and so on.
  - `itt inspect`, `itt show` and the hook's opening context show the relations that need attention.
  - `itt doctor` reports invalid relations.
- Rules: a pair of Intents has at most one active relation; an Intent has at most one parent; relations never form cycles; an ended Intent gains no new parts.
- `itt decision create … --from ID` records the Intent a Decision came from. Decisions still apply globally, and without `--from` a Decision is project-level.
- The Skill teaches agents when a relation or a source is worth recording, and when it is not.

## IntHub

- A new Flow page draws Intents as a graph of curved lines on a time axis. Parts and derived goals branch off their parent, successors continue a lane, and dependencies and conflicts are marked with their state. Decisions appear as pins under their source Intent, and project-level ones in a band at the top. Intents without relations are listed below the graph. Ended goals can be hidden, or the view focused on one Intent; on phones it opens on the most recent open goal.
- The Intent sheet lists relations in both directions with their states and links to the Intent's place in the Flow page. The Decision sheet shows its source Intent.
- The continuation brief and its copied Markdown mark parents, blocked dependencies, unresolved conflicts and pending handovers, and the health summary counts dependency-blocked goals.
- The timeline loads in pages. The overview now carries only the newest 20 Snaps, and search returns at most 50 matches with the total.

## Performance

- `itt pull` no longer slows down with the square of history size. 4,000 Snaps install in 2.0 s instead of 14.7 s, and 20,000 in about 11 s instead of an estimated six minutes.

## Upgrading

- Re-run the installer, or `pipx install --force "intent-history @ git+https://github.com/dozybot001/Intent.git@v7.2.0"`.
- Pulling a history in which an existing Intent later gained a relation needs itt 7.2; 7.1 rejects it in its fast-forward check. Snaps and other written fields stay as strict as before.
